# Episode One Authority 2: Day One AAA Production Control Specification

**Status: PROTECTED CANON — GATE ONE IMPLEMENTATION AUTHORIZED; ALL OTHER GATES LOCKED**

This is the second of three Episode One design authorities approved by the
creator (Nexus/Kit) on 9 August 2026. It must not be condensed, rewritten,
reinterpreted, or prematurely implemented beyond its authorized gate.

## Scope of this authority

This specification governs how Day One is built. It defines, as binding
production requirements:

- Persistent 3D world behaviour.
- Continuous player control.
- NPC continuity and schedules.
- Character physical performance.
- Disability representation (per the locked six-character framework).
- Personal Faience behaviour.
- Spatial/binaural audio.
- Accessibility equivalence across sensory and control needs.
- Stable world geometry.
- Environmental storytelling.
- State persistence.
- Anti-shortcut rules.
- Seven gated implementation stages.

AAA is a **player-facing quality target, not permission to fake scope.** If an
approved requirement cannot be implemented faithfully, the conflict must be
flagged to the creator instead of silently substituting something easier.

## The seven gates

Implementation proceeds through seven gated stages. Each gate must be
explicitly authorized by the creator before any of its work begins. As of
9 August 2026:

- **GATE ONE — AUTHORIZED.** Earth transport through physical boarding of the
  Hearth. Full requirements below.
- **Gates Two through Seven — LOCKED.** Their content (Medical, Faience
  connection, F, quarters, café, work, Captain check-in, and everything after)
  is protected future canon only. Do not implement, prototype, or partially
  implement any of it until explicitly authorized.

## Gate One requirements (authorized 9 August 2026)

Gate One builds the approved Earth transport through physical boarding of the
Hearth, as a vertical-slice quality gate. It must:

1. Preserve the existing protected Resonance spatial/world model and existing
   accessibility systems.
2. Deliver continuous physical movement — never room/location cards.
3. Provide a populated lunar concourse with independent ambient NPC activity.
4. Provide comm-badge contextual spatial guidance.
5. Include optional memorial discovery without quest-marker treatment.
6. Maintain a stable spatial layout.
7. Deliver physical boarding-bridge traversal.
8. Provide layered positional/spatial audio.
9. Provide accessibility equivalence across sensory and control needs.
10. Carry environmental storytelling.

The player has already selected one of the six authored playable characters
before this opening; automatic reactions may differ appropriately by
character.

### Gate One prohibitions (vertical-slice discipline)

- No dialogue wheels.
- No destination menus.
- No location cards.
- No stationary exposition NPCs.
- No generic text-scene substitutions.
- No parallel navigation system.

## Not authorized (protected future canon)

Medical, Faience connection, F, quarters, café, role work, Captain check-in,
later days, the Day Four blackout, the attack, evacuation, pods, C, E, and
Episode Two. These remain documentation-only until the creator explicitly
authorizes their gates.
