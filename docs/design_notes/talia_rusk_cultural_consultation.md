# Talia Rusk — Indigenous Cultural Consultation Process

Status: canon. This design note records the consultation process for Security Lead Talia Rusk's Indigenous cultural representation, resolving the process gap identified in the 2026 Q3 studio readiness review (Issue 6.2). The consultation ownership question is resolved; her cultural specifics remain intentionally unspecified until reviewed under this process.

## Who is responsible for arranging the consultation

The Nexus System/project team handles this directly. The team are First Nations themselves and take responsibility for cultural consultation, research, and determining when additional community-specific expertise is needed.

The team's own Indigenous identity does not mean it automatically claims authority over every Indigenous nation, people, or tradition. If Talia's specific nation/community falls outside the team's own lived or community knowledge, the team will seek appropriate nation/community-specific consultation rather than generalising from its own experience.

## What form the consultation takes

Ongoing cultural review throughout development rather than a one-time sensitivity check.

The Nexus System/project team reviews Talia's character documents, cultural development, dialogue, visual representation, family/community relationships, traditions, material culture, historical references, and gameplay implementation.

Additional community-specific consultation is sought whenever material goes beyond the team's own cultural knowledge or authority.

## Minimum outcome before her cultural specifics can be written

Before culturally specific material becomes locked canon, Talia's specific nation/community must be established and appropriately researched, and the following reviewed:

- Naming.
- Family and kinship relationships.
- Traditions.
- Language where applicable.
- Community relationships.
- Material culture.
- Visual representation.
- Historical references.
- Dialogue.
- Cultural values.
- Gameplay representation.

Binding constraints during development:

- Do not invent unresolved cultural details as placeholders.
- Do not substitute generic "Indigenous culture" for a specific nation's culture.
- Do not assume one Indigenous nation or consultant represents another.

## Timing

Cultural review must occur before the affected material becomes player-facing.

Talia may appear in beta before every cultural detail is finalised if that beta does not require unresolved cultural specifics. Any unresolved cultural material remains intentionally unspecified until reviewed. This process should not unnecessarily block unrelated beta development.

## Relationship to other documents

- The Canon Rules document's Real Cultural Influence Is Documented as Lineage rule governs how any reviewed cultural material is recorded once approved.
- The presentation bible's Character Identity Map and Cultural Presentation documents carry Talia's representation once specifics are approved.
- The Open Questions document tracks the remaining unresolved item: which nation/community Talia belongs to.
- The Decision Log records the date this process was adopted.
