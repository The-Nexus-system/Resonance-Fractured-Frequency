CANON RULE — KNOWLEDGE IS HISTORICAL

The repository records objective canon.

Characters record subjective knowledge.

These are not always identical.

Documents written from an in-universe perspective may contain:

Incomplete observations.

Incorrect conclusions.

Competing scientific theories.

Political bias.

Cultural assumptions.

Historical misunderstanding.

Missing evidence.

These documents remain canonical because they accurately represent what the author knew at the time.

As new Operations, expeditions and perspectives are experienced, humanity's understanding grows.

Knowledge is corrected through discovery rather than retcon.

The universe itself remains internally consistent.

The player's understanding evolves.
