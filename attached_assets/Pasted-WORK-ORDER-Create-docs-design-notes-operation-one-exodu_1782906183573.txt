Purpose:

Create the definitive chronological timeline for Operation One: Exodus.

This document becomes the master source of truth for every playable perspective.

Every future campaign, chapter, mission, conversation, rescue, flashback and cinematic must remain consistent with this timeline.

This document defines what objectively happened.

Perspective campaigns define how the player experienced those events.

--------------------------------------------------

CANON RULE

There is only one timeline.

There are many perspectives.

--------------------------------------------------

Timeline Scope

Begin seven days before the destruction of CSV Hearth.

End when the player regains consciousness inside the damaged escape pod at the beginning of Chapter Two.

Cover every day.

Cover every major shift.

Cover the attack minute-by-minute.
 --------------------------------------------------

For every major event include:

Approximate time.

Location aboard Hearth.

Characters present.

Characters absent.

Departments involved.

Whether the player witnesses the event in the Maintenance campaign.

Whether the event is visible from other campaigns.

Whether the event becomes important later.

--------------------------------------------------

Include recurring daily events such as:

Morning announcements.

Shift changes.

Meals.

Maintenance windows.

Engineering inspections.

Medical rounds.

Research meetings.

Training exercises.

School activities.

Community gatherings.

Emergency drills.

Evening routines.

Night shift.

--------------------------------------------------

Perspective Crossovers

Mark every event where two or more future protagonists cross paths.

These crossover points become reusable scenes for every campaign.

The same event should be experienced differently depending upon the player's profession.

--------------------------------------------------

Canon Rule

Nothing in a later campaign may contradict this timeline.

Later campaigns reveal additional context.

Never alternate history.

This document becomes the master continuity reference for the entire Resonance franchise.
