# Day Three

## Theme

Investment.

Stewardship.

Civilization.

Hope.

Day Three marks the point where the player stops thinking about Hearth as something they inherited.

They begin thinking about what they want to leave behind.

For the first time, the player actively shapes the future of the station rather than simply maintaining the present.

The central lesson of Day Three is:

Civilizations are not preserved accidentally.

They are deliberately built every single day.

---

# Morning

## 05:30

Night crews complete another routine shift.

Engineering reports all primary systems nominal.

Maintenance closes every overnight work order.

Security reports another incident-free night.

The anomaly now appears consistently every night.

Its duration has increased.

No one yet recognizes the pattern.

## 06:30 Morning Announcement

Captain Elian of the Vale System.

"This is Captain Elian of the Vale System.

Good morning everyone.

Today I'd like you to notice something.

Everything around us exists because someone believed tomorrow was worth preparing for.

Someone planted today's gardens.

Someone designed today's classrooms.

Someone repaired today's elevators.

Someone taught today's teachers.

Someone trained today's doctors.

Someone built today's home.

Someday...

Someone else will inherit what we build today.

Let's leave them something worth inheriting.

Have a wonderful morning."

Morning music begins.

Artificial sunrise gradually illuminates Hearth.

The player immediately recognizes dozens of familiar sounds.

No tutorial is necessary.

Hearth now feels instinctively familiar.
