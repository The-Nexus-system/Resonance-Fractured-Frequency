# Operation One: Exodus

# Master Timeline

## Purpose

This document defines the objective chronology of Operation One: Exodus.

Every playable campaign follows this timeline.

Campaigns never alter history.

Campaigns reveal different perspectives of the same history.

There is only one canon timeline.

There are many valid perspectives.

---

# Canon Rules

## Canon Rule

Every campaign depicts the same historical events.

No campaign changes history.

Each campaign reveals information unavailable to the others.

Events overlap naturally.

Together, all campaigns tell one complete story.

---

## Canon Rule

Every playable protagonist exists simultaneously.

When the player controls one protagonist, every other protagonist continues performing their own duties elsewhere aboard CSV Hearth.

The player may witness them.

The player may interact with them.

The player may never prevent them from performing their responsibilities.

---

## Canon Rule

Every major event must occur at exactly the same point in time in every campaign.

Dialogue may differ.

Perspective may differ.

Knowledge may differ.

The event itself never changes.

---

## Canon Rule

Every campaign contains crossover moments.

The player regularly witnesses other future protagonists performing their jobs.

When replaying another campaign, those same scenes become playable from the opposite perspective.

---

## Canon Rule

No campaign contains information the protagonist could not reasonably know.

The audience gains understanding through multiple playthroughs.

The characters do not possess impossible knowledge.

---

## Canon Rule

The player never becomes the sole hero.

CSV Hearth survives as long as it does because hundreds of people perform their jobs simultaneously.

The player contributes.

The player does not single-handedly save the ship.

---
