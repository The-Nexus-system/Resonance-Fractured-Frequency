Can we survive together?

# Operation One: Exodus
# Chapter Three
## Community
Primary Question
Can we survive together?
Chapter Answer
Yes.
But only if everyone contributes.
Ending Question
What are we willing to build together?
--------------------------------------------------
## Purpose
Chapter Three transforms the rescued survivors into a functioning community.
Chapter Two taught rescue.
Chapter Three teaches cooperation.
The player is no longer responsible for saving isolated people.
The player is responsible for helping very different people live together inside a fragile new home.
The chapter introduces long-term progression systems.
The player learns that civilization is built through relationships rather than resources.

I also want to make one recommendation before we build the detailed parts.

Don’t make Chapter Three feel like “base management.”

Make it feel like living.

Think of Mass Effect’s Normandy between missions, Red Dead Redemption 2’s camp, or Star Trek: The Next Generation when there isn’t a crisis. Those quieter moments are where players become attached to the people.

I think Chapter Three should be the chapter where players stop thinking, “these are NPCs,” and start thinking, “this is my crew.” That’s what will make Chapters Four through Seven matter emotionally.
