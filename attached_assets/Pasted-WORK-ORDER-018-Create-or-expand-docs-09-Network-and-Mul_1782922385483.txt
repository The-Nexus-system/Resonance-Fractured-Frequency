PURPOSE

Multiplayer exists to strengthen community.

It is never required to experience the complete story.

Every campaign must remain fully playable solo.

Multiplayer should enhance cooperation rather than create dependency.

----------------------------------------------------------------

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Resonance is cooperative by design.

Players succeed by sharing knowledge.

Sharing professions.

Sharing perspectives.

Sharing accessibility.

Sharing responsibility.

The game should reward communication rather than mechanical perfection.
