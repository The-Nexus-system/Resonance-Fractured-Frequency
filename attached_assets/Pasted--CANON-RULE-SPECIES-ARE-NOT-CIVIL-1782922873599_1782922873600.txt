----------------------------------------------------------------

CANON RULE — SPECIES ARE NOT CIVILIZATIONS

Resonance distinguishes between species and civilizations.

These terms are never interchangeable.

Species describe biology.

Civilizations describe society.

A species answers:

"What kind of living being is this?"

A civilization answers:

"How do these people choose to live together?"

----------------------------------------------------------------

SPECIES

A species defines biological characteristics.

Examples include:

Physiology.

Evolution.

Genetics.

Sensory systems.

Communication abilities.

Development.

Reproduction.

Average lifespan.

Environmental adaptations.

Medical considerations.

Accessibility needs.

A species does not determine:

Government.

Culture.

Religion.

Morality.

Politics.

Economics.

Art.

Language.

Architecture.

Values.

Those belong to civilizations.

----------------------------------------------------------------

CIVILIZATIONS

A civilization defines how people organize themselves.

A civilization may include:

One species.

Multiple species.

Artificial intelligences.

Synthetic life.

Hybrid communities.

Civilizations define:

Government.

Law.

Education.

Healthcare.

Research.

Engineering.

Architecture.

Economics.

Trade.

Language.

Art.

Music.

Storytelling.

Celebrations.

Community traditions.

Accessibility standards.

Diplomacy.

Emergency response.

Historical memory.

Shared values.

Civilizations evolve over time.

Species do not determine civilization.

----------------------------------------------------------------

MULTIPLE CIVILIZATIONS

A single species may develop many civilizations.

These civilizations may differ in:

Language.

Government.

Beliefs.

Technology.

Accessibility.

Architecture.

Traditions.

Relationships.

Scientific priorities.

Political systems.

Conflict resolution.

No civilization represents an entire species.
 ----------------------------------------------------------------

MULTI-SPECIES CIVILIZATIONS

A civilization may include multiple intelligent species.

Residents cooperate through shared institutions rather than shared biology.

Public spaces, transportation, education, healthcare, communication systems and emergency planning should be designed to support everyone living within that civilization.

Accessibility is expected to accommodate differences both within and between species.

----------------------------------------------------------------

CANON PHILOSOPHY

No species exists solely to be an enemy.

No civilization exists solely to create conflict.

Every civilization should feel capable of producing:

Scientists.

Teachers.

Children.

Artists.

Engineers.

Doctors.

Maintenance workers.

Caretakers.

Musicians.

Leaders.

Volunteers.

Families.

Ordinary citizens.

The Resonance universe is populated by societies, not stereotypes.

Conflict arises from history, circumstance, competing needs and individual decisions—not from biology alone.
