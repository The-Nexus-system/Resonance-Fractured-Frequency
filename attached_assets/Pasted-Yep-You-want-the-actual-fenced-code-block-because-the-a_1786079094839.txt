TALIA RUSK — INDIGENOUS CULTURAL CONSULTATION
1. Who is responsible for arranging the consultation?
The Nexus System/project team will handle this directly. We are First Nations ourselves and will take responsibility for cultural consultation, research, and determining when additional community-specific expertise is needed.
Our own Indigenous identity does not mean we automatically claim authority over every Indigenous nation, people, or tradition. If Talia's specific nation/community falls outside our own lived or community knowledge, we will seek appropriate nation/community-specific consultation rather than generalizing from our own experience.
2. What form will the consultation take?
Ongoing cultural review throughout development rather than a one-time sensitivity check.
The Nexus System/project team will review Talia's character documents, cultural development, dialogue, visual representation, family/community relationships, traditions, material culture, historical references, and gameplay implementation.
Additional community-specific consultation should be sought whenever material goes beyond our own cultural knowledge or authority.
3. What is the minimum outcome required before her cultural specifics can be written?
Before culturally specific material becomes locked canon, we must establish and appropriately research Talia's specific nation/community and review:
- naming
- family and kinship relationships
- traditions
- language where applicable
- community relationships
- material culture
- visual representation
- historical references
- dialogue
- cultural values
- gameplay representation
Do not invent unresolved cultural details as placeholders.
Do not substitute generic "Indigenous culture" for a specific nation's culture.
Do not assume one Indigenous nation or consultant represents another.
4. Rough timing?
Cultural review must occur before the affected material becomes player-facing.
Talia may appear in beta before every cultural detail is finalized if that beta does not require unresolved cultural specifics.
Any unresolved cultural material remains intentionally unspecified until reviewed.
This should not unnecessarily block unrelated beta development.
