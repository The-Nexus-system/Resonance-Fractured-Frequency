# Part Six

## Expedition Consequences

### Purpose

Complete the player's decision framework for exploration.

Every major expedition should conclude with meaningful decisions.

Every decision changes future gameplay.

No decision permanently removes content.

Different choices create different histories.

---

## Decision Four

Salvage Capacity

The player's inventory is nearly full.

Not everything can be recovered.

The player must decide what leaves Hearth first.

---

Choice A

Prioritize People

Examples

Medical records.

Personal journals.

AAC devices.

Family photographs.

Recorded messages.

Adaptive equipment.

Immediate Result

Less technology recovered.

Future Effect

Crew relationships deepen.

Knowledge Archive expands.

Historical understanding improves.

Future dialogue becomes richer.

---

Choice B

Prioritize Technology

Examples

Fabricators.

Medical devices.

Power systems.

Engineering equipment.

Scientific instruments.

Immediate Result

Station capabilities expand rapidly.

Future Effect

Community conversations about Hearth develop more slowly.

Some personal stories remain undiscovered until later expeditions.

---

Choice C

Prioritize Knowledge

Examples

Research databases.

Educational materials.

Historical archives.

Navigation records.

Scientific observations.

Immediate Result

Knowledge Archive grows substantially.

Future Effect

Research and diplomacy progress sooner.

Construction progresses more gradually.

---

Choice D

Balanced Recovery

Recover smaller amounts from every category.

Immediate Result

Steady progress across every gameplay system.

Future Effect

No single system advances rapidly.

Future expeditions remain highly flexible.

---

## Decision Five

Unexpected Survivor Signal

A weak emergency beacon briefly activates.

Its source cannot be confirmed.

Continuing toward it risks delaying the expedition.

---

Choice A

Investigate immediately.

Immediate Result

Additional exploration.

Possible rescue.

Future Effect

The expedition becomes longer.

Additional resources are consumed.

Optional future storyline begins.

---

Choice B

Mark the location.

Immediate Result

Precise coordinates recorded.

Future Effect

Dedicated rescue mission becomes available later.

Current expedition remains on schedule.

---

Choice C

Ignore for now.

Immediate Result

Mission continues uninterrupted.

Future Effect

The signal becomes an ongoing discussion aboard the station.

Its fate remains unknown until another opportunity arises.

---

## Decision Six

Damaged Area

A damaged section blocks further exploration.

---

Choice A

Repair immediately.

Immediate Result

Current expedition continues.

Resources consumed.

Future Effect

Area permanently accessible.

Later expeditions become easier.

---

Choice B

Find another route.

Immediate Result

Exploration continues.

Additional time required.

Future Effect

Alternative passages become permanently mapped.

Hidden discoveries become available.

---

Choice C

Return later with better equipment.

Immediate Result

Current expedition ends sooner.

Future Effect

Future engineering upgrades unlock additional exploration opportunities.

---

## Decision Seven

Mission Completion

The player has completed the primary objective.

Several optional discoveries remain.

---

Choice A

Leave immediately.

Immediate Result

Safe return.

Earlier station development.

Future Effect

Remaining discoveries await later expeditions.

Crew praises disciplined decision making.

---

Choice B

Remain until supplies reach minimum safety limits.

Immediate Result

Additional discoveries.

Additional risk.

Future Effect

Greater rewards.

More maintenance required after returning.

---

Choice C

Continue until every accessible area has been searched.

Immediate Result

Maximum exploration.

Highest resource expenditure.

Future Effect

Future expeditions to this area become shorter.

Crew fatigue increases.

Additional recovery time required before the next expedition.

---

## Canon Rule

Every expedition should conclude with the player believing they could have discovered even more.

Completion should inspire curiosity.

Not exhaustion.

Players should feel excited to return.

Not relieved to leave.

---

## Chapter Four Completion

The player now fully understands the expedition gameplay loop.

Preparation.

Planning.

Exploration.

Decision making.

Recovery.

Reflection.

Community growth.

These systems become the foundation for every exploration chapter that follows throughout Resonance.
