# Part Two

## The First Community Meeting

### Purpose

Teach collaborative leadership.

Teach long-term planning.

Teach that communities function because people solve problems together.

The player does not attend this meeting to receive orders.

The player attends to become part of the discussion.

The meeting should feel closer to a town council than a military briefing.

---

### Narrative Purpose

This is the first time every primary crew member has occupied the same room since the destruction of Hearth.

The immediate emergency has ended.

The future now depends upon planning rather than reacting.

Every person has different priorities.

None of those priorities are wrong.

---

### Gameplay Purpose

Introduce:

Community planning.

Long-term station projects.

Project priorities.

Shared decision making.

Crew consultation.

Mission planning.

Station needs.

Consensus building.

The player learns that planning itself is gameplay.

---

### Meeting Structure

The Vale System opens the meeting.

Different members naturally front as different topics arise.

No explanation is given.

This is simply how command functions.

The meeting follows a predictable structure.

Current status.

Immediate concerns.

Long-term projects.

Open discussion.

Community requests.

Player input.

Final decisions.

---

### Crew Reports

Rowan reports:

Structural integrity.

Maintenance backlog.

Repair priorities.

Fabrication capacity.

Hidden maintenance concerns the player discovered earlier alter his report.

If the player completed preventative inspections, Rowan has fewer urgent repairs to discuss.

---

Mara reports:

Power reserves.

Engineering capacity.

Upgrade opportunities.

Energy shortages.

If the player visited Mara first earlier that morning, additional engineering options become available because the player already understands her diagnostic terminology.

---

Samira reports:

Crew health.

Mental wellbeing.

Medical inventory.

Fatigue.

Preventative care priorities.

If the player visited Samira first, optional wellness projects become available.

Crew morale begins slightly higher.

---

Jonah reports:

Supply inventory.

Mission readiness.

Construction schedules.

Outstanding requests.

If the player organized tasks earlier, Jonah's report reflects improved efficiency.

---

Talia reports:

Security concerns.

Hazard assessments.

Emergency preparedness.

External risks.

If the player previously completed environmental assessments, fewer unknown risks remain.

---

Soren reports:

Research progress.

Questions requiring investigation.

Knowledge Archive updates.

Strange signal observations.

If the player documented additional observations earlier, Soren incorporates those discoveries into future research.

---

Mira reports:

Not formally.

Instead she interrupts.

She asks questions.

Those questions expose assumptions adults have made.

At least one optional project should become available because of something Mira notices.

Curiosity changes planning.

---

### Player Decisions

The player is asked to help establish the station's first major priorities.

The player does not choose one "correct" answer.

Instead the player determines what receives attention first.

Choice One

Expand living space.

Immediate Result:

Construction begins.

Crew comfort increases.

Future Result:

Higher morale.

Additional community conversations.

Earlier access to optional social activities.

Construction projects progress more slowly.

---

Choice Two

Strengthen engineering systems.

Immediate Result:

Power reliability improves.

Engineering upgrades unlock sooner.

Future Result:

Advanced equipment becomes available earlier.

Living conditions improve more slowly.

---

Choice Three

Increase medical preparedness.

Immediate Result:

Medical inventory expands.

Crew health improves.

Future Result:

Future injuries recover faster.

Medical research becomes available sooner.

Other construction projects progress more slowly.

---

Choice Four

Expand research capabilities.

Immediate Result:

Knowledge Archive develops faster.

New discoveries occur earlier.

Future Result:

Additional dialogue options.

Earlier diplomatic advantages.

Engineering and construction improvements occur more gradually.

---

Choice Five

Strengthen station defenses.

Immediate Result:

Security systems improve.

Future exploration becomes safer.

Future Result:

Earlier defensive upgrades.

Reduced accidental damage.

Scientific progress slows slightly.

---

Choice Six

Search immediately for additional survivors.

Immediate Result:

Exploration projects begin sooner.

Additional optional rescue opportunities appear earlier.

Future Result:

The station develops more slowly because resources are shared with rescue operations.

---

### Canon Rule

None of these choices create a better or worse playthrough.

Every priority eventually becomes available.

The player's choice determines what the community accomplishes first.

History changes.

Canon does not.

---

### Crew Reactions

Every crew member responds differently to each priority.

Nobody is universally pleased.

Nobody is universally disappointed.

Respectful disagreement is healthy.

The player begins understanding personalities through discussion rather than exposition.

---

### Gameplay Systems Introduced

Station project board.

Priority management.

Community voting.

Long-term planning.

Crew consultation.

Project dependencies.

Project timelines.

Community requests.

---

### Accessibility

Every discussion topic is reviewable after the meeting.

Players may replay:

Entire meeting.

Individual reports.

Player decisions.

Crew recommendations.

Meeting minutes.

No information should be permanently lost because a player became distracted or missed dialogue.

---

### Emotional Goal

The player should leave the meeting feeling trusted.

They are no longer simply another survivor.

They have become one of the people helping shape the future.

---

### End Condition

The first major community project officially begins.

Construction starts.

The station begins changing because of the player's decision.

The player is asked to report to Rowan, where practical work on the chosen project begins in Part Three.
