------------------------------------------------------------

PURPOSE

Resonance uses modular architecture.

Rooms are reusable.

Buildings are reusable.

Neighborhoods are reusable.

Entire ships are assembled from standardized architectural modules.

Campaigns should reference modules rather than recreating rooms.

------------------------------------------------------------

CANON RULE

A room exists independently of the ship containing it.

Only its location changes.

Its purpose remains consistent.
