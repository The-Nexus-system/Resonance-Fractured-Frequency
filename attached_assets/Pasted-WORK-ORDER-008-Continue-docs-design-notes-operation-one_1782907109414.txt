# Day Seven

# Part Three

## Theme

Home.

Family.

Community.

Belonging.

The workday is ending.

Life aboard Hearth shifts from profession to community.

The player should begin seeing people outside their jobs.

This is where Hearth becomes a home rather than simply a workplace.

## 17:00

### Shift Change

Day shift hands responsibilities to evening crews.

Maintenance completes outstanding work orders.

Engineering begins lower-impact system diagnostics that are easier to perform during reduced station activity.

Medical transitions from scheduled appointments to walk-in care and overnight preparation.

Security shifts from workplace patrols toward public gathering spaces.

Research archives the day's observations and prepares overnight experiments.

The station noticeably relaxes.

## Narrative Purpose

Teach the player that Hearth is more than infrastructure.

It has traditions.

Relationships.

The player should finish the evening believing:

"I could live here."

That feeling is essential.

The destruction of Hearth must feel like losing a home rather than leaving a tutorial area.
