# Part Three

## First Expedition

### Purpose

Teach the player how exploration functions throughout Resonance.

Teach that exploration is not about collecting resources.

Teach that every location tells a story.

Teach that preparation affects the player's experience without preventing success.

This establishes the core exploration gameplay loop used throughout the remainder of the campaign.

---

### Narrative Purpose

The player arrives at the outer edge of Hearth's debris field.

The navigation systems automatically identify several possible expedition sites.

These sites are not randomly generated.

Each location existed before the destruction of Hearth.

Each location has history.

Each location has purpose.

The player decides where to begin.

Every location eventually becomes explorable.

The player's decision determines which story is experienced first.

---

### Gameplay Purpose

Introduce:

Zone selection.

Environmental storytelling.

Mapping.

Exploration logs.

Mission recording.

Recoverable history.

Recoverable technology.

Recoverable culture.

Recoverable accessibility technology.

---

### Exploration Philosophy

Every location should answer one question.

Every location should create additional questions.

Every location should provide:

Something useful.

Something emotional.

Something historical.

Something mysterious.

Resources alone are never sufficient rewards.

### Canon Rule

Every expedition zone eventually becomes required.

The player chooses exploration order.

Not exploration completion.

Different playthroughs emphasize different discoveries first.

Canon remains unchanged.

---

### Exploration Gameplay

Every location contains multiple categories of discoveries.

Recoverable technology.

Recoverable history.

Recoverable accessibility innovations.

Recoverable cultural artifacts.

Recoverable environmental recordings.

Recoverable scientific observations.

Recoverable personal stories.

Recoverable maintenance records.

Players are encouraged to investigate every category.

None are mandatory.

Curiosity is rewarded.

---

### Environmental Storytelling

No location should explain itself through exposition.

Instead players learn through observation.

Examples include:

Coffee mugs left on desks.

Wheel tracks through corridors.

Braille signage.

AAC terminals.

Children's drawings.

Maintenance notes.

Half-finished repairs.

Open books.

Musical instruments.

Gardens.

Personal decorations.

These details should communicate that Hearth was lived in.

---

### Dynamic Discoveries

Every expedition includes optional discoveries.

Examples include:

An emergency beacon still transmitting.

A maintenance drone continuing its programmed route.

A service animal quietly waiting beside its handler.

An unfinished engineering project.

A music rehearsal preserved in environmental recordings.

A classroom lesson interrupted by evacuation.

A community garden still growing under emergency lighting.

The player chooses whether to investigate.

Ignoring optional discoveries never prevents chapter completion.

Investigating them permanently expands the Knowledge Archive and deepens community understanding.

---

### Advisor System

The chosen advisor continues guiding the player.

However, every crew member may occasionally contribute remotely if their expertise becomes relevant.

The station remains part of the expedition.

The player never feels alone.

---

### Accessibility

Every environmental clue must exist through multiple sensory channels.

Visual.

Audio.

Captions.

Braille.

Tactile feedback.

Environmental descriptions.

Players should never depend upon one sensory modality to understand a location.

---

### Emotional Goal

The player should realize that Hearth was never simply a spacecraft.

It was a city.

A home.

A school.

A hospital.

A workplace.

A neighborhood.

Exploration should feel like walking through memory rather than ruins.

---

### End Condition

The player successfully completes their first exploration zone.

Before returning home, they receive a notification.

Multiple additional locations have now been identified.

The player realizes that they have explored only a tiny portion of Hearth.

The journey home begins, leading into Part Four.
