# Part Four

## The First Tests

### Purpose

The player has spent Chapter Three learning how a community functions.

This section asks the player to demonstrate that learning.

The player must complete every test before Chapter Three can end.

The order is chosen by the player.

Each completed test permanently reinforces a gameplay system introduced earlier in the chapter.

Each test also demonstrates why every crew member remains essential.

No crew member solves their own test alone.

The player participates in every solution.

---

### Gameplay Philosophy

These are not examinations.

These are ordinary problems experienced by a growing community.

Nothing is world-ending.

Nothing is a boss battle.

These moments teach that civilization survives through countless small acts of cooperation.

---

## Test One

### Rowan

Problem

A newly completed habitat module develops small structural problems.

Player Learns

Preventative maintenance.

Player Decision

Repair immediately.

Or

Continue observing before committing resources.

Immediate repair consumes resources now but prevents future failures.

Observation saves resources now but creates more repair work later.

Neither choice is wrong.

The player learns maintenance is about balancing risk.

---

## Test Two

### Mara

Problem

Power demand exceeds projections after recent construction.

Player Learns

Power management.

Player Decision

Reduce power to non-essential systems.

Or

Reroute power and accept temporary instability.

Or

Delay expansion until additional generation is available.

Each approach changes future engineering priorities.

---

## Test Three

### Samira

Problem

Several crew members are exhausted after continuous construction.

No one is seriously injured.
 Player Learns

Communities require recovery.

Player Decision

Continue construction.

Schedule mandatory rest.

Rotate work shifts.

Different choices alter project timelines and crew morale.

The player learns that efficiency is not always measured by speed.

---

## Test Four

### Jonah

Problem

Three important projects now compete for the same resources.

Player Learns

Prioritization.

Player Decision

Determine which project receives available materials first.

The remaining projects wait.

Nothing is cancelled.

Only delayed.

The player's priorities begin shaping the station's history.

---

## Test Five

### Talia

Problem

Routine inspection identifies several preventable safety hazards.

Player Learns

Risk assessment.

Player Decision

Correct every hazard before work resumes.

Or

Accept limited risk in order to maintain construction schedules.

Future incidents reflect the player's philosophy toward safety.

---

## Test Six

### Soren

Problem

An unusual material recovered from Hearth cannot be identified.

Player Learns

Scientific method.

Player Decision

Experiment immediately.

Research existing records first.

Ask other crew members for observations before forming conclusions.

Different approaches unlock different Knowledge Archive entries.

Curiosity is rewarded.

Rushing assumptions is not.

---

## Test Seven

### Mira

Problem

Mira notices something everyone else overlooked.

The observation seems insignificant.

The player chooses whether to investigate.

Player Learns

Never dismiss curiosity.

If investigated:

The player discovers an accessibility improvement, environmental shortcut, hidden resource or future story thread.

If ignored:

The opportunity remains available later, but the community misses an immediate improvement.

The lesson is that valuable ideas can come from anyone.

---

## Test Eight

### The Vale System

Problem

The previous seven tests produce conflicting priorities.

Not everyone agrees.

Player Learns
 Leadership.

Player Decision

Before making a final decision, the player must speak with every primary crew member.

Every crew member explains:

Their concern.

Their recommendation.

Their reasoning.

Only after hearing every perspective may the player make a final decision.

The player's decision establishes the station's immediate direction moving into Chapter Four.

---

### Completion Requirement

All eight First Tests must be completed before the chapter can end.

No crew member may be skipped.

Every completed test permanently expands the player's gameplay abilities.

Every completed test reinforces one of the core philosophies of Resonance:

Community.

Accessibility.

Curiosity.

Adaptation.

Shared knowledge.

Hope.

---

### Chapter End

The station successfully overcomes its first ordinary challenges.

The player has demonstrated competence in every core community system.

The crew is no longer simply surviving together.

They are functioning together.

As normal routines begin to resume...

The unknown signal returns.

This time it is stronger.

Longer.

Intentional.

Someone—or something—knows the station exists.

End Chapter Three.
