CANON RULES

Characters continue living their lives whether or not the player is present.

Characters never wait for the player.

Characters never exist solely to hand out quests.

Professional responsibility always comes before plot convenience.

Every recurring character has a complete daily routine.

Every recurring character has favorite places aboard Hearth.

Every recurring character has recurring habits.

The player should eventually know where someone is likely to be simply by understanding who they are.
