CANON RULE

Every major expedition begins and ends at home.

The player's home serves as the emotional, mechanical and narrative anchor of Resonance.

Every expedition follows the same narrative rhythm:

Prepare.

Depart.

Explore.

Recover.

Return.

Reflect.

Rebuild.

The player should always return home changed.

The station should always change because of what the player brought home.

The player never returns with resources alone.

Every expedition must return with at least one meaningful contribution to the community.

Meaningful contributions include:

A survivor.

Knowledge.

History.

Technology.

Cultural artifacts.

Accessibility improvements.

Engineering discoveries.

Medical discoveries.

Relationships.

Hope.

Resources support these discoveries.

Resources are never the primary reward.

--------------------------------------------------

CANON RULE

Every expedition must answer one important question.

Every expedition must create at least two new questions.

Understanding should continually expand.

Curiosity should always outpace certainty.

The player should finish every expedition knowing more than before while realizing there is still far more left to discover.

# Operation One: Exodus

# Chapter Four

## Salvage

### Primary Question

Can we build something worth protecting?

### Chapter Answer

Yes.

By remembering what came before.

### Ending Question

What really happened aboard Hearth before its destruction?

--------------------------------------------------

## Purpose

Chapter Four introduces the expedition gameplay loop that will define the remainder of Operation One.

For the first time, the player deliberately leaves home.

Not because of an emergency.

Because the community has chosen to explore.

The player is no longer escaping Hearth.

The player is returning to Hearth.

This chapter introduces exploration, mapping, environmental storytelling and recovery.

The player learns that salvaging history is just as important as salvaging technology.

# Part One

## Preparing For The First Expedition

### Purpose

Teach expedition planning.

Teach that preparation determines success.

Teach that leaving home is a community effort rather than a solo activity.

The player should never simply press a button labeled "Start Mission."

Every expedition begins through conversation, planning and preparation.

--------------------------------------------------

### Narrative

Several days have passed since the completion of the station's first major expansion.

Life has become more predictable.

Morning routines exist.

Construction continues.

The community is stable enough to begin exploring.

During the morning briefing, the crew agrees it is finally time to return to Hearth.

Not to reclaim it.

To learn from it.

--------------------------------------------------

### Community Discussion

Every primary crew member contributes.

Rowan wishes to recover tools and fabrication equipment.

Mara wishes to recover engineering systems and power infrastructure.

Samira wishes to recover medical supplies and patient records.

Jonah wishes to recover logistics data and station maps.

Talia wishes to secure safe expedition routes.

Soren wishes to investigate unusual readings from the wreckage.

Mira asks whether anyone left messages behind.

The Vale System moderates discussion while encouraging every voice to be heard.

--------------------------------------------------

### Player Decisions

The player must make three decisions before departure.

These choices change the expedition.

They do not change canon.

--------------------------------------------------

Decision One

Select Expedition Priority

Choice A

Engineering Recovery

Primary Goal

Recover fabrication equipment and engineering components.

Immediate Benefits

Additional engineering resources.

New fabrication recipes.

Engineering research progresses faster.

Future Effects

Construction projects require fewer salvaged materials.

Advanced station upgrades unlock earlier.

--------------------------------------------------

Choice B

Medical Recovery

Primary Goal

Recover medical equipment and patient archives.

Immediate Benefits

Expanded medical inventory.

Additional rehabilitation equipment.

Medical Archive entries.

Future Effects

Future injuries recover more quickly.

Preventative healthcare projects expand sooner.

--------------------------------------------------

Choice C

Historical Recovery

Primary Goal

Recover personal records, educational archives and cultural artifacts.

Immediate Benefits

Knowledge Archive expands significantly.

Additional optional conversations.

Historical discoveries.

Future Effects

Diplomatic understanding.

Additional exploration clues.

Greater emotional understanding of Hearth.

--------------------------------------------------

Choice D

Infrastructure Recovery

Primary Goal

Recover station systems and habitat modules.

Immediate Benefits

Additional construction materials.

Habitat expansion.

Life support redundancy.

Future Effects

Larger station modules become available sooner.

Community growth accelerates.

--------------------------------------------------

Decision Two

Preparation Focus

Choice A

Pack additional tools.

Benefits

Additional repair options during exploration.

Reduced equipment failures.

Less inventory space for recovered items.

--------------------------------------------------

Choice B

Pack additional medical supplies.

Benefits

Improved emergency response.

Safer exploration.

Reduced cargo capacity.

--------------------------------------------------

Choice C

Pack additional research equipment.

Benefits

Additional environmental discoveries.

Expanded Knowledge Archive.

Reduced available space for engineering equipment.

--------------------------------------------------

Choice D

Travel light.

Benefits

Maximum salvage capacity.

Less flexibility when unexpected problems arise.

--------------------------------------------------

### Accessibility

All preparation choices must remain fully reviewable before departure.

Players may change selections until confirming launch.

Mission summaries must clearly explain:

Benefits.

Trade-offs.

Recommended crew expertise.

Accessibility considerations.

No player should be required to memorize preparation choices.
