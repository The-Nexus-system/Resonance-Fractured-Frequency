PURPOSE

The Universe Bible defines every known intelligent species and civilization.

It explains:

Who exists.

Where they live.

How they communicate.

How they govern themselves.

How they build.

How they learn.

How they remember.

How they cooperate.

How they disagree.

How they explore.

How they interact with humanity.

How they interact with one another.

Every campaign, operation, novel, expansion and future civilization must reference this document.
 ------------------------------------------------------------

CANON RULE

Species are not factions.

Species are biological or artificial peoples.

Civilizations are social, political and cultural organizations.

A single species may contain many civilizations.

A civilization may include multiple species.

Do not treat an entire species as sharing one culture or one government.
