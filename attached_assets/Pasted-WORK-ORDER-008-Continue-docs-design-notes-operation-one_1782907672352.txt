# Day Five

# Part Nine

## Theme

Reflection.

Tradition.

Belonging.

By now Hearth no longer feels like a ship.

It feels like a hometown.

The player should begin developing emotional routines that have nothing to do with gameplay objectives.

These ordinary rituals become some of the strongest memories after Hearth is lost.

Every evening aboard Hearth develops its own personality.

Residents gather because they want to.

Adaptive sports.

Public astronomy.

Board games.

## Accessibility

The player notices that accessibility has become culture.

Parents automatically introduce themselves verbally before conversations.

Meeting agendas exist in print, braille and digital formats before meetings begin.

Sign language naturally appears throughout public spaces.

AAC users participate without interruption.

Children imitate multiple communication styles during play.

Nobody teaches inclusion.

Children simply grow up surrounded by it.

## Environmental Storytelling

Residents begin decorating common areas for the upcoming weekend community festival.

Fresh produce from Hydroponics is shared freely during dinner.

The public thank-you wall continues growing.

Maintenance tools remain neatly organized because everyone returns borrowed equipment.

The station feels deeply lived in.
